Go to Students, click Add Student, fill in the form and click Save. The new student will appear in the table.

On the Students page, use the two dropdown menus next to the search bar to choose a field (ID, Name, Programme, Block, or Room) and sort order (Ascending or Descending).

Open Complaints and click directly on the status pill (Pending, In Progress, or Resolved) in the table row to change it immediately.

Click the Export CSV button on the top right of the Students or Complaints table to download a spreadsheet-ready file.
